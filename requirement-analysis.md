# Insurance Management Platform 

## Project Objective:The Insurance Management Platform is a comprehensive web-based application designed to simplify and digitize the management of insurance operations.

## User Roles
### Administrator
- Manage customers
- Manage insurance policies
- Verify and approve/reject claims
- Monitor premium payments
- View reports and dashboard statistics
### Insurance Agent
- Register customers
- Create insurance policies
- Submit and verify claims
- Upload required documents
- Track customer policies
### Customer
- View policy details
- Pay premiums
- Upload required documents
- Submit insurance claims
- View claim and payment history


## Modules
- Customer Management
- Policy Management
- Claim Management
- Premium Tracking
- Document Management
- Reports Dashboard

## Application Workflow
1. Register a new customer.
2. Create an insurance policy for the customer.
3. Record premium payments.
4. Upload customer and policy documents.
5. Submit insurance claims.
6. Verify and approve or reject claims.
7. Track policy status, payments, and claims.
8. View reports and dashboard statistics.


## Functional Requirements
- Register and manage customer information.
- Create, renew, and cancel insurance policies.
- Submit and process insurance claims.
- Record and track premium payments.
- Upload and manage insurance documents.
- Generate dashboard reports and business statistics.
- Search customers and policies efficiently.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- User-friendly interface
- Responsive design
- Secure user authentication
- Fast search and data retrieval
- Reliable data storage
- Proper validation and error handling
- Scalable application architecture
